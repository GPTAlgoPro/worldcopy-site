# WorldCopy (摹界) Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

WorldCopy (whose source and internal bundle components may use the name
“Snap3D”) is designed to capture, reconstruct, generate, view, and export 3D
models on your device. It does not require an account and does not include
advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated upload service.

## Data WorldCopy uses on your device

WorldCopy may use the following data only to provide features you choose:

- camera images and video;
- LiDAR depth, ARKit environment meshes, camera poses, and intrinsics;
- device motion, gravity, and gyroscope samples associated with a capture;
- photos or videos you explicitly select with Apple's system picker;
- prompts, subject photos or four-view images you explicitly select, generated 3D models,
  previews, quality reports, and local performance diagnostics.
- on Mac, `.wc3d` packages you explicitly open or drop into WorldCopy.

This user content is processed inside the app sandbox on your device.
Apple manages separately downloaded hosted model assets where that delivery
path is available; Mac model packs live inside the app sandbox. Neither is a
capture, prompt, image, or model created by you. WorldCopy does not
automatically transmit your content to the developer or a third-party server.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Completed models and their retained capture material remain on your device
until you delete them in WorldCopy or remove the app. You can choose whether
new models retain their original capture data. Incomplete or interrupted work
may be kept locally for recovery and is limited or cleaned by the app's stated
retention rules. Temporary export and sharing files are removed after use or
by the app's cleanup process.

On Mac, a selected `.wc3d` source is not modified. After integrity validation,
WorldCopy stores a private project copy under Application Support; temporary
import staging is deleted on failure or cancellation.

WorldCopy provides storage and recovery controls in Settings and the model
library. Deleting a model removes the local data described by the deletion
confirmation. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain data. On Mac, deleting the application may leave its data container behind. Delete unwanted projects in the app first. Exported copies and system backups must be managed separately.

## Sharing and external apps

WorldCopy does not upload your content to its provider. Content is handed to
another app only when you explicitly export, save, copy, or share it, or when
you choose to open another app or website. Opening an AI image app from the
prompt guide first copies the prompt you approved to the system clipboard.
The receiving app, service, or website then handles that data under its own
privacy policy.

## Permissions

Camera, world-sensing, and motion permissions support on-device capture and 3D
reconstruction. Add-only Photos permission is requested only when you ask to
save a composed image. Photo and video imports use Apple's system picker so
WorldCopy does not request access to your full photo library.
The Mac app does not capture with a camera; it uses App Sandbox
user-selected-file access only when you choose or drop a package.

## Third-party software

WorldCopy includes third-party libraries and uses separate Apple-hosted,
on-device AI resources for the modeling or texturing features you choose. A
resource downloads only after you select the corresponding feature and accept
its terms. These resources do not add an automatic user-content collection
channel. Third-party license notices are available inside the app.

## Children

WorldCopy is not directed specifically to children and knowingly collects no
personal data from children or other users.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published at this URL and reflected in
the app where appropriate. For privacy or support questions, open an issue at
<https://github.com/GPTAlgoPro/worldcopy-site/issues>. Do not include private capture
data or other sensitive information in a public issue.

# 摹界隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 20 日

摹界（源代码和部分内部组件可能使用“Snap3D”名称）用于在设备本机采集、重建、
生成、查看和导出 3D 模型。App 不要求账户，不包含广告、分析、跟踪，也没有由
开发者运营的自动上传服务。

## App 在本机使用的数据

只有在你主动使用对应功能时，摹界才可能在本机使用：

- 相机照片和视频；
- LiDAR 深度、ARKit 环境网格、相机位姿和内参；
- 与一次采集关联的设备运动、重力和陀螺仪样本；
- 你通过 Apple 系统选择器明确选取的照片或视频；
- 提示词、你明确选择的主体照片或四视图图片、生成的 3D 模型、预览、质量报告及本机
  性能诊断。
- 在 Mac 上由你明确打开或拖入摹界的 `.wc3d` 文件包。

这些用户内容在 App 沙盒内处理。采用 Apple 托管交付时，另行下载的模型资源由系统
管理；Mac 模型包保存在 App 沙盒内。它们都不属于你的采集、提示词、图片或生成模型。
摹界不会自动把你的内容传给开发者或
第三方服务器。

## 存储、保留与删除

完成的模型及选择保留的采集材料会留在本机，直到你在摹界中删除，或从系统卸载
App。你可以选择新模型是否保留原始采集。未完成或中断的工作可能为了恢复而暂存，
并受 App 明示的保留数量或清理规则限制。临时导出和分享文件会在使用后或由清理
流程删除。

在 Mac 上，摹界不会修改你选择的 `.wc3d` 源文件；完整性校验后，私有项目副本保存
在 Application Support，失败或取消的导入 staging 会被删除。

摹界在“设置”和模型库中提供存储与恢复管理。删除模型时，确认页会说明将删除的
本机数据；在 iOS 上删除 App 会移除本机 App 数据，但“卸载 App”可能保留数据。在 Mac 上仅删除应用程序可能保留其数据容器，请先在 App 内删除不再需要的项目。已导出的副本和系统备份需要单独管理。

## 分享与外部 App

摹界不会把你的内容上传给服务提供者。只有当你明确执行导出、保存、复制、分享，
或主动打开其他 App、网站时，内容才会交给接收方。从提示词助手打开 AI 图像 App
前，摹界会先把你确认的提示词复制到系统剪贴板。接收方随后按其自己的隐私政策
处理数据。

## 权限

相机、世界感知和运动权限用于本机采集与 3D 重建。只有当你要求保存合成图片时，
App 才申请“仅添加照片”权限。照片和视频导入使用 Apple 系统选择器，因此摹界不
申请读取整个照片图库。
Mac App 不使用相机采集；只有当你选择或拖入文件包时，才使用 App Sandbox 的
“用户选择文件”访问权限。

## 第三方软件

摹界包含第三方程序库，并为你所选的建模或贴图功能使用相互独立的 Apple 托管端侧 AI 资源。
只有当你选择对应功能并接受相关条款后，才会下载对应资源；这些资源不会增加自动收集用户内容的通道。
相关许可声明可在 App 内查看。

## 儿童

摹界并非专门面向儿童，也不会主动收集儿童或其他用户的个人数据。

## 变更与联系

本政策的重大变更会发布在本网址，并在需要时同步到 App。隐私或支持问题请在
<https://github.com/GPTAlgoPro/worldcopy-site/issues> 提交。公开问题中请勿附带私人采集
数据或其他敏感信息。
