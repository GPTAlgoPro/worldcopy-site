WORLDCOPY / macOS

More room
for your next creation.

From capture reconstruction to AI creation, bring editing, inspection and delivery into one workspace.

01

Continue from iPhone

Open or drop a .wc3d capture package. WorldCopy validates it and saves a private project copy without changing the source file.

02

One photo or four views

Create from a single image or front, back, left and right views, then choose the matching texture workflow.

03

Edit, inspect, export

Work on the model canvas, inspect topology, UVs, materials and scale, and export supported formats including USDZ, GLB and OBJ.

Made for Apple silicon

Requires macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon and at least 16 GB unified memory. AI features also check available memory, storage and resource status. Download the engines you need, then process on-device.

Available in Simplified Chinese and English. .wc3d transfers projects as files; it does not provide automatic cloud sync.

A new dimension for your photo.

Create a model from a photo. Save the shape, then choose its textures. Process your work on-device and decide how to share it.

  1. Choose a photo
  2. Generate a model
  3. Add optional textures
  4. Preview and deliver

AI modeling and texturing are Beta features. iPhone / iPad require at least 8 GB RAM, including 8 GB. AI and engine downloads are disabled below this threshold; Pro does not remove the limit. Mac has separate hardware requirements. Supported devices must download the engines before use.
