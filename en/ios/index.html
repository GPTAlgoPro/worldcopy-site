WORLDCOPY / iOS

Everyday inspiration.
Made three-dimensional.

Object scanning, single-photo AI modeling and visual sharing, designed for iPhone.

01

Scan a real object

On devices that support Apple Object Capture, follow the framing, capture and reconstruction guidance.

02

One photo to a model

Choose a photo or photograph an object. Check the prepared subject before starting on-device AI modeling.

03

Save first. Add texture next.

Save the completed mesh, then optionally create PBR texture. Cancellation or texture failure preserves the completed model.

04

Place your work in a photo

Compose a model into your own photo, or photograph it in AR on supported devices. Save and share the finished picture.

Devices & resources

Requires iOS / iPadOS 26 or later. Scanning and AR depend on runtime device support; iPad does not necessarily offer the same capture capabilities as iPhone. AI modeling and PBR require the corresponding engine resources and sufficient available memory and storage. Temperature, region and resource status also affect availability.

Available in Simplified Chinese and English. AI results and processing time vary with input and device. Generated geometry is not a measurement of the real object.
